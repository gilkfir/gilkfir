Searching

When you search, you activate a filter — your mind narrows to match what it already expects to find. You see only confirmations. You absorb nothing new. Searching tightens perception. It makes you less receptive, not more.

“It happens quite easily that he only sees the thing that he is seeking.”

— Siddhartha, Hermann Hesse
3 / 5 @gilkfir

Finding

Finding is a different state entirely. To find, you must be open — not looking for something specific. Finding happens when you release the goal. It expands perception. It lets reality surprise you.

“Finding means: to be free, to be receptive, to have no goal.”

— Siddhartha, Hermann Hesse
4 / 5 @gilkfir

They don’t share a spectrum.
They require different states of being.

Searching
  • narrows
  • filters
  • driven
  • goal-obsessed
Finding
  • opens
  • receives
  • present
  • goal-free

You don’t find by searching harder.
You find by learning when to stop searching.
